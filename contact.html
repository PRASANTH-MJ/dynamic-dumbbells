FAQ

Questions

Do you have a separate space for women?

Yes — Dynamic Diva is our dedicated women's fitness studio with ladies-only programs and group classes.

What are your timings?

Monday to Saturday, 5:00 AM – 12:00 PM and 4:30 PM – 9:00 PM. Call us for holiday timings.

Can I try before joining?

Yes — book a free trial on the home page and get a free assessment and workout.

Is personal training included in membership?

PT is a separate add-on. Half-yearly and yearly members get free PT sessions included.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Floor trainers will teach you the basics and we run a dedicated beginner batch.

Can I freeze my membership?

Quarterly and longer plans can be paused for travel or medical reasons — ask at the front desk.
